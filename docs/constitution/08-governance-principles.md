# Chapter 8 — Governance Principles

Governance is how the platform makes and enforces decisions at scale. It keeps the Vision intact as the team, codebase, and ecosystem grow.

## 8.1 The Constitution is supreme

- The Constitution outranks any single product decision, any single pull request, and any single individual.
- Conflicting documents are amended; the Constitution is not silently overridden.

## 8.2 Governing bodies

| Body | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Founder / Owner** | Holds the Vision and Mission; final authority on constitutional amendments. |
| **Platform Council** | Architecture, product, and governance decisions across suites; reviews maturity levels; resolves conflicts. |
| **Suite Maintainers** | Own a suite's code, quality, metrics, and roadmap within the Constitution. |
| **Contributors** | The wider community; propose changes, raise issues, and participate in reviews. |

## 8.3 Decisions are recorded

- Significant decisions are recorded as **Architecture/Product Decision Records** (ADRs) in the repository.
- A decision without a record is a rumour. Records capture: context, options considered, decision, consequences.
- Maturity level changes (Chapter 10) are recorded decisions with evidence.

## 8.4 Transparency

- Roadmaps, decisions, and maturity levels are visible in the repository and, over time, on the Developer Portal and Community suites.
- Post-mortems are blameless, written, and shared.
- Security disclosures are handled responsibly and documented.

## 8.5 Compliance and law

- The platform operates under the laws of Tanzania and every jurisdiction it serves, including data protection obligations (e.g. the PECA Act, 2022).
- Data protection, payment, and consumer protection compliance are treated as product requirements.
- We publish policies (privacy, terms) and honour them.

## 8.6 Financial governance

- Money movement, fees, and reconciliations are governed by the Finance suite and audited.
- Any feature touching user funds requires financial review before release.

## 8.7 Change and amendment

- Constitutional and governance changes follow the Amendment Process (Constitution README).
- Code changes follow the Engineering Principles (Chapter 5) and review rules.
- No individual — including founders — can bypass recorded governance without a recorded decision.

## 8.8 Review cadence

- Quarterly: platform review of suites, maturity levels, and roadmap alignment with the Vision.
- Annually: constitutional review; amendments proposed as needed.
- Continuously: pull-request reviews and decision records.
