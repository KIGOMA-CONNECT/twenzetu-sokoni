# Chapter 3 — Mission

## Our mission

> **To build the trusted commerce infrastructure of Africa — making it simple, safe, and profitable for any person or organization to buy, sell, and grow, through a platform engineered to global standards and rooted in African context.**

## What we do (the verbs)

- **Connect** — buyers, vendors, service providers, and communities through a multi-tenant marketplace.
- **Enable** — commerce across every channel that matters: web, Android, and USSD.
- **Protect** — money, data, and reputation through secure-by-default engineering.
- **Grow** — with our users: the small vendor who becomes a large business is our measure of success.
- **Standardize** — enterprise-grade APIs, documentation, and processes that partners can build on.

## What we deliver today

A live, production multi-tenant marketplace (`twenzetusokoni.com`) with:

- vendor and product management,
- orders and order tracking,
- service listings and transport/cargo requests,
- user identity, authentication, and roles,
- tenant isolation and per-tenant configuration,
- audit, finance, and resilience foundations,
- payment processor integrations (M-Pesa, AzamPay) being activated,
- Android delivery via Trusted Web Activity with push notifications,
- USSD channel for feature-phone access.

## How we measure mission success

| Measure | Question it answers |
|---|---|
| Active vendors | Are real businesses selling? |
| Completed orders | Is commerce actually happening? |
| Successful payments | Is money moving safely? |
| Repeat usage | Do users come back and trust us? |
| Tenant growth | Are organizations adopting the platform? |
| Platform quality | Do our metrics, tests, and audits show reliability? |

## Mission and the Constitution

Every chapter that follows exists so that this mission survives growth. The Vision (Chapter 2) tells us where we are going; the Mission tells us what we do along the way.
