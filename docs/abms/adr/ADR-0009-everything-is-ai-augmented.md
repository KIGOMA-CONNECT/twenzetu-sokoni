# ADR-0009: Everything is AI Augmented

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-26
- **Decision maker:** Owner
- **Context:** AI should not be a separate module or feature. It should be a capability that enhances every platform.
- **Options considered:**
  1. AI as a standalone module
  2. AI as an integrated capability across all platforms (chosen)
- **Decision:** AI is not a feature. AI is a capability. Every platform has its own AI: Planning, Finance, HR, CRM, Innovation, Healthcare, Government. AI augments human decision-making, not obscures it.
- **Consequences:** Intelligence Layer is a cross-cutting concern. Every platform exposes structured data suitable for AI. AI decisions are explainable. Human override is always available.
- **Constitution check:** Chapters 5 (Engineering Principles), 6 (Architecture Principles)
