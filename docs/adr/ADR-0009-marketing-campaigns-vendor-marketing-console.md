# ADR-0009: Marketing campaigns (tenant-managed SMS broadcasts) and vendor marketing console

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-18
- Decision maker: Owner + Platform Council
- Context: ADR-0006 made marketing content data (taglines, benefits, emoji, adverts) and exposed it through public APIs, but the tenant had no way to *push* promotions: no one-to-many broadcast, no per-tenant copy management console, and no deliverable channel for scheduled campaigns. Logistics L4 (Cargo) shipped server-derived driver earnings and OTP-confirmed delivery, but customers still had no way to rate a driver from their order list (only from the live tracking screen). We need (1) a tenant-managed campaign aggregate that sends one message to the active customer base over SMS with per-message send/failure accounting, (2) a vendor-facing marketing console covering adverts, campaigns and category copy, and (3) driver rating surfaced in Order History to close the last consumer-side gap of logistics L4.
- Options considered:
  1. **Frontend-only "campaign" that opens WhatsApp/social composer** — no server record, no audience targeting, no delivery accounting, not tenant-scoped. Rejected.
  2. **Campaign aggregate + SMS dispatch through the existing `ISmsService` router (chosen)** — the campaign is a DRAFT aggregate with name/message/channel/status; launch resolves the audience from the tenant's ACTIVE CUSTOMER users (capped at 500/message to bound cost), marks the campaign ACTIVE, sends one SMS per phone via the per-country router, records sent/failed counts, and settles COMPLETED. A second per-tenant management path (PATCH `/categories/:id/marketing`) reuses the existing `ProductCategory.updateMarketing` for tagline/benefits/emoji. Driver rating is wired to the existing `POST /driver-reviews` use-case, which derives the delivery from the order.
  3. **Full scheduler + WhatsApp + audience segmentation engine** — higher fidelity but needs a job queue, a WhatsApp provider (none exists yet), and segmentation rules; deferred to Marketing L4.
- Decision:
  1. **New `marketing_campaigns` table** (tenant-aware): `name`, `message`, `channel` (sms only today), `audience_type` (all_customers), `status` (DRAFT/ACTIVE/COMPLETED/FAILED/CANCELLED), `sent_count`, `failed_count`, `total_audience`, `scheduled_at`, `started_at`, `completed_at`. Created by migration `1700000000039-AddMarketingCampaigns`.
  2. **Domain & use-cases.** `MarketingCampaign` aggregate (`create`/`launch`/`recordResult`/`complete`/`cancel`); `IMarketingCampaignRepository` adds `findAudiencePhoneNumbers` (raw SQL over `users` where `role='CUSTOMER'` and `status='ACTIVE'`, distinct phone, limit 500). Use-cases: `CreateMarketingCampaignUseCase` (drafts, rejects whatsapp channel), `ListMarketingCampaignsUseCase`, `LaunchMarketingCampaignUseCase` (DRAFT→ACTIVE, resolves audience, dispatches via `ISmsService.send`, records results, COMPLETED).
  3. **API.** JWT-protected `/api/marketing/campaigns` (POST create, GET list, POST `:id/launch`). Campaign launch is synchronous; the per-message loop is bounded by the 500-recipient cap. `PATCH /api/categories/:id/marketing` updates tagline/benefits/emoji per tenant.
  4. **Vendor marketing console.** New `/vendor/marketing` page lists campaigns with Launch, adverts (create/list), and per-category copy editing. Route is `ProtectedRoute roles={['vendor']}`, nav item shown to vendors with `manage_products`.
  5. **Logistics L4 consumer gap.** Order History now offers "Rate Driver" on DELIVERED orders, posting to the existing `POST /driver-reviews` (delivery derived from order), complementing the rating card already on the tracking screen.
- Consequences:
  - Tenants can broadcast SMS promotions without a deploy, with an audit trail of send/failure counts and a bounded per-campaign cost ceiling (500 messages).
  - SMS channel is the only supported channel; WhatsApp campaigns are rejected at creation until a provider is added (Marketing L4).
  - Launch runs synchronously in the request; a 500-recipient blast is acceptable today but a queue/background worker is the natural next step (flagged for L4).
  - Driver ratings are now reachable from both the tracking screen and order history, closing consumer-facing logistics L4.
- Constitution check: Trust (Ch4), Product Principles 7.2/7.3 local-first Swahili content (Ch7), Architecture Principle 6.8 standards over invention (Ch6), tenancy isolation preserved for all write/tenant-scoped endpoints (Ch2), fail-closed defaults retained for every non-public endpoint (Ch9), cost-conservative defaults for money-facing channels (Ch5).
