# AfriMarket Engineering Institute

> Enterprise products do not grow by code alone. They grow because people know how to use them and build on them. This is why there are thousands of SAP consultants, Microsoft Certified Professionals, Salesforce architects, and Oracle consultants — not because the software is huge, but because they built an ecosystem of knowledge.

The AfriMarket Engineering Institute is the knowledge engine of the platform. It turns AfriMarket from a codebase into a profession.

## Mission of the Institute

To create, certify, and spread AfriMarket expertise — internally and across partners, developers, and the wider ecosystem — so that the platform is understood, adopted, and extended with global standards.

## Pillars

### Research
- Studies market needs, sector patterns, and technology direction for the platform.
- Produces the evidence behind maturity level changes (L0–L7).
- Publishes findings through the Documentation and Community suites.

### Architecture
- Owns the architectural standards behind the [Enterprise Suites](enterprise-suites.md) model.
- Maintains the reference architecture and the platform's decision records (Chapter 9).
- Reviews cross-suite architecture as the Platform Council's technical arm.

### Certification
- Defines and administers formal credentials for AfriMarket professionals (see tracks below).
- Keeps certification aligned with the Constitution and current platform behaviour.

### Training
- Delivers structured learning: onboarding for the internal team, academies for partners and developers.
- Provides the AfriMarket Learning platform experience.

## Academies

- **Developer Academy** — engineers building on AfriMarket: platform architecture, API integration, suite development, security.
- **Partner Academy** — implementation partners: deploying, customizing, and supporting AfriMarket for end customers.

## Certification tracks

| Track | Audience | Demonstrates |
|---|---|---|
| **AfriMarket Solution Architect** | Architects & consultants | Designing solutions across suites that fit the platform architecture |
| **AfriMarket Developer** | Engineers | Building and integrating on AfriMarket APIs correctly and securely |
| **AfriMarket Implementation Partner** | Partner organizations | Delivering and operating AfriMarket deployments to a certified standard |

Each track has defined requirements, assessments, and renewal criteria. Details evolve in the Learning suite; the mandate is constitutional.

## Why this matters for AfriMarket

1. **Trust.** Certified professionals raise the quality of every deployment and integration.
2. **Ecosystem.** Partners extend the platform into sectors we cannot serve alone.
3. **Talent.** The Institute grows the local engineering and consulting profession in Africa.
4. **Moat.** SAP and Salesforce are not just software — they are ecosystems. The Institute builds ours.
